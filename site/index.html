How to Play

HOW IT WORKS

$POKEMONBANK is USDC-paired on pump.fun. Trade fees fill the card vault. Holders are trainers. Trainers catch cards.

1

The vault fills

Every buy and sell on $POKEMONBANK generates USDC fees that flow into the card vault automatically.

2

Trainers are entered

If you hold $POKEMONBANK, you're in. Every holder is entered into every draw. No signup, no staking.

3

A card is pulled

When the vault covers the floor price, the bot pulls a Pokemon card NFT from the marketplace using the accumulated USDC.

4

A trainer catches it

One random holder is selected. The card lands directly in their wallet. On-chain, verifiable, yours forever.
